Hi, I'm Johannes. I'm Head of Operations at [Prometis Lab](https://www.prometislab.org/), a spin-off of Ghent University and BNP Paribas Fortis, where I lead software development. In my own time I write cycling software that combines noisy sensor data and physics into live numbers to help you read the road ahead.

I studied physics, then did a [PhD in economics](https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/01JZ0FKKB552K6A14DEKSEET9P) at Ghent University on how households react to income changes. Most of that PhD went into the pipeline that turns raw pseudonymised bank transactions into a research-ready dataset, and that pipeline now runs at Prometis Lab every day. It's the kind of work I like most: a messy source of data, a model of what it measures, and software that others can build on.

Some things I've started:

- [Barberfish](https://github.com/jpweytjens/barberfish): native-feeling data field enhancements for the Hammerhead Karoo, such as a steady live grade fitted over the last 30 m of road
- Godot: a gradient-aware cycling ETA estimator, in progress
- [vanzelfsprekend](https://vanzelfsprekend.johannesweytjens.be): Matplotlib plots that speak for themselves, inspired by Tufte and Doumont
- [Ghent Festive 500](https://ghentfestive500.be): free social rides with Ghent's cycling clubs between Christmas and New Year

Publications and blog posts are on [johannesweytjens.be](https://johannesweytjens.be).
